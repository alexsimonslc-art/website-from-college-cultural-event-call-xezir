Welcome to the Arena

Combat is the premier annual cultural festival designed to push your creative and intellectual boundaries to the absolute limit. From the high-octane synchrony of Western and Group Dance to the high-pressure hot seats of the Stress Interview, we host a battlefield for every talent. Whether you are an artist, a debater, a photographer, or a corporate maven, your stage is waiting.

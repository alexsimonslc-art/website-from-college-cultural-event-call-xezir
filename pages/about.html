The Vision & The Arena

Combat is not just an event; it's a rite of passage. Born at Loyola College, Chennai, Combat was envisioned as a raw, high-stakes platform where the finest creative minds and academic giants from all over the country clash. What started as an intramural face-off has evolved into South India’s ultimate intercollegiate cultural showdown.

We blend arts, music, and performance with rigorous professional trials. Whether it's the chaotic campus rush of the Treasure Hunt or the razor-sharp scrutiny of our Stress Interview panel, every single event is meticulously balanced to reward authentic technique, fierce dedication, and raw passion.
